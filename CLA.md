# FlyTerm Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to FlyTerm. This agreement clarifies the
intellectual-property license granted with contributions.

## Why this exists

FlyTerm is released under the GNU General Public License v3. The same codebase
is also distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

GPLv3's installation-information requirements (§6) conflict with the App
Store's distribution terms — this is the well-documented reason VLC was once
pulled from the store. As the sole copyright holder the project owner can grant
themselves an exception. Once third-party contributions are merged, that
exception no longer covers the whole work, and store distribution would become
non-compliant.

This CLA resolves exactly that, and nothing more. **You keep the copyright to
your contribution.**

4. **No warranty.** The contribution is provided as-is, without warranty of

Your contribution remains available to everyone under the GPLv3, exactly like
the rest of the project.
